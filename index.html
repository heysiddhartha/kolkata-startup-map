Kolkata Startups, Companies & Jobs

Kolkata Startup Map is a public-source directory and interactive map of startups, companies, agencies, founders and hiring opportunities across Kolkata and West Bengal. Explore companies by sector and locality, find current public job listings, discover founders and CEOs, and follow funding, incubation, accelerator, university and ecosystem updates.

Kolkata Startup Map is designed for three practical searches: discover a company, find a current hiring signal, or understand where a sector is concentrated. Each profile keeps public-source evidence separate from verification and location confidence. Hiring information is time-sensitive, while company and founder information is enriched as better sources become available.

Use the directory for company discovery, the jobs section for public roles and the founders directory for people building Kolkata businesses. Sector and locality pages provide focused entry points for search engines and readers, while the methodology page explains how records are sourced and checked.

The map is built from public company, hiring, founder and ecosystem sources rather than a single official register. A company being listed does not by itself mean it is active, funded or currently hiring. Hiring signals can change quickly, so important job details should always be checked at the original application source.

Kolkata Startup Map — common questions

What can I find here?

You can discover Kolkata companies and startups by sector and locality, browse public hiring opportunities, explore founders and CEOs, and follow startup ecosystem news, resources and programmes.

Are the listings official?

No. The map is an independent public-source directory. Verification status, source links and freshness signals are kept visible so users can check important information themselves.

Can I add or correct a company?

Yes. Use the Add startup flow to submit a company or correction. New submissions are held for review before they become public listings.
