# Kolkata Startup Map — data notes

The current directory uses public ecosystem discovery sources and area-level map points. It deliberately distinguishes directory records from live job records.

- eChai currently reports 204 startups on its Kolkata map.
- eChai's Kolkata startup grid currently exposes 96 companies in its startup section.
- NDITA reports 188 IT/ITES companies in its Sector V establishment survey.

Job records in the current UI were sourced from public LinkedIn search/listing pages on 27 September 2026. Listings can expire, so they are linked to their source rather than represented as permanently live.

The production roadmap is to verify every company, resolve official career pages, geocode verified offices, and attach last-checked timestamps to vacancies.
