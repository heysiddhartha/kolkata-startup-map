Privacy

Last updated: September 2026

Kolkata Startup Map is a public directory of companies, startups, jobs and ecosystem activity. This page describes the basic information handling used by the site.

Startup submissions

If you submit a company, the information you provide can include the company name, website, founder, contact email, sector, locality, LinkedIn URL, careers URL and description. Submissions are held for review after basic validation, and a listing is published only after approval. Public-source verification and exact map location are kept separate.

How information is used

Submitted information is used to create and maintain a public directory listing. Public company, job and ecosystem information may also be collected from public sources and linked back to those sources.

Third-party services

The site uses GitHub Pages for hosting, Supabase for directory data and submission processing, and public map/source services. Those services have their own privacy and data policies.

Browser storage

The site stores the selected light/dark theme in browser local storage. Browsing the public directory does not require an account.

Corrections and removal

If a company wants information corrected or removed, use the site's submission flow or contact the maintainer through the project profile.

Important note

Company and job information can change. Verify important details at the linked company or application source before relying on them.
