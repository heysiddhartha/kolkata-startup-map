Privacy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Kolkata Startup Map is a public directory of companies, startups, sectors and public hiring signals. This page describes the information collected through the site.

Startup submissions

If you submit a company, we receive the information you provide, such as company name, website, founder, contact email, sector, locality, LinkedIn URL, careers URL and description. Submissions are reviewed before publication.

How information is used

Submitted information is used to review, maintain and publish the directory. Public company and job information may also be collected from public sources and linked back to those sources.

Third-party services

The site uses GitHub Pages for hosting, Supabase for directory data and submission processing, and third-party map and public-source services. Those services may process technical request information according to their own policies.

Corrections and removal

If a company wants information corrected or removed, please use the public contact details associated with the project or submit an update request through the site.

Important note

Company and job information can change. The map is an informational directory, not an employment guarantee or official registry. Verify important details at the linked company or application source.
